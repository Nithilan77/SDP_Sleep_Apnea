## 1. Project definition

### 1.1 Goal
A wearable sleep-apnea **screening** system (not a diagnostic device) that fuses two signals from **one chest-worn sensor**: **ECG** and **respiratory effort** (chest-wall motion). It flags people who should get a clinical sleep study. The scientific claim: fusing heart + breathing beats using either alone. (`CLAUDE.md` §1–§2.) Motivation as recorded there: sleep apnea affects ~1 in 7 adults; ~80% of moderate–severe cases are undiagnosed; polysomnography (PSG) is expensive and lab-bound; ECG-only detection tops out ~85–88% and cannot distinguish apnea type.

### 1.2 Team and context
Student project (two students: Nithilan and Guru) with an industry collaboration (ST Microelectronics), guided by faculty (Dr. A. Shahina, Dr. K. S. Gayathri). Part of the larger "Pinnacle" sleep-analysis programme; a sister team does EEG-free sleep *staging* (Sleep-EDF dataset — a **different** track and dataset, not ours). (`CLAUDE.md` §3.)

### 1.3 Hardware
- **ST SensorTile.box PRO** + **STEVAL-MKI242A** adapter; 5 units.
- The adapter exposes **QVAR** (electrostatic sensor channel + dry-electrode analog front-end). QVAR is wired to chest electrodes (Lead-II placement, 3-tab cable) and is **always ECG** in this project.
- The onboard **IMU** (accelerometer + gyro + temperature) records chest-wall motion = respiratory effort, simultaneously.
- Nominal QVAR/IMU rate **240 Hz**; **empirically 238.1–243.2 Hz** across all in-house files (`results/inhouse_validation/quality_report.csv`; 243.10 Hz in the breath-hold session). Project rule 8: never trust the nominal rate; `src/ingest/sensortile.py` computes it from the microsecond timestamp.
- A Muse S Athena EEG headband exists in the ecosystem but belongs to the staging track.
- File formats: old firmware = tab-delimited `.txt`, 16 columns (`Timestamp [us]`, `A_X/Y/Z [LSB]`, `[mg]`, `G_X/Y/Z [LSB]`, `[dps]`, `T [LSB]`, `T [degC]`, `QVAR [LSB]`); new firmware (2026-10) = comma-delimited `.csv` (`time[us]`, `acc_*[LSB]`, `acc_*[mg]`, `gyro_*[LSB]`, `gyro_*[dsp]` (firmware's label for dps), `temp[LSB]`, `temp[C]`, `ah_qvar[LSB]`). Both load through `src/ingest/sensortile.py::load_sensortile`.

### 1.4 The core constraint
Our own subjects are healthy 19–20-year-olds who essentially do not have apnea. Therefore (`CLAUDE.md` §4):
- Training data must be **public, expert-labelled clinical data**: PhysioNet Apnea-ECG (ECG branch) and MESA via NSRR (fusion model).
- Our recordings are **validation only**: healthy overnight recordings as negative controls, voluntary awake breath-holds as safe, labelled apnea-*like* events.
- Any experiment that trains on our own recordings is "wrong by design" — **with one explicit, scoped exception below.**

**The one exception (recorded 2026-10-09, in `docs/PROGRESS.md` §7f, *before any result existed*):** an advisor-directed supervised step fits **only linear (L2 logistic-regression) heads** on (a) frozen MESA-trained CANet features and (b) 8 hand-crafted effort features, using the 7 labelled holds of the single 2026-10-07 breath-hold session. No encoder, attention or MESA-head weight is updated; no deep network is trained on in-house data (the lesson of §7d); the healthy in-house nights remain validation-only; the ECG (PhysioNet) and MESA branches are unchanged. Reason: after four label-free adaptation attempts failed (§3.1), a labelled signal from our own hardware was the missing ingredient, and a linear head is the only capacity the data can support. With one subject, no subject-independent claim is possible; numbers are within-subject leave-one-event-out.

### 1.5 Architecture (reproduced from `CLAUDE.md` §7)
```
                       ┌─ ECG BRANCH (trained on PhysioNet) ──────────────────┐
QVAR/ECG ─bandpass─▶ R-peaks (Pan–Tompkins) ─▶ RR-interval series
                       ─▶ CVHR features ─▶ [classical baseline | 1D-CNN] ─▶ P(apnea)/min
                                                                                  │
                       ┌─ EFFORT BRANCH (IMU, signal processing only) ─────┐      ▼
IMU ─▶ accel magnitude ─▶ motion-mask ─▶ bandpass 0.08–0.6Hz               ─▶ DECISION FUSION
    ─▶ Hilbert envelope ─▶ breathing rate + effort amplitude               ─▶      │
                                                                                  ▼
                                                  confident event / flagged / rejected
                                                                                  ▼
                                            per-night report: events, AHI-like rate, severity
```
Why decision-level fusion: the branches train on different data and decision-level fusion keeps each interpretable. **As built** (`docs/PROGRESS.md` §1, §7b): R-peaks come from `neurokit2` (not a hand-written Pan–Tompkins); the *fusion model actually trained and validated* is feature-level (CANet: two streams with cross-attention) on MESA belts, not the decision-level combiner of `src/fusion/` (which does not exist as code — Phase 6 on our own hardware is blocked, §5). Key terms: CVHR = cyclic variation of heart rate (bradycardia during apnea, tachycardia on resumption); AHI = events/hour (<5 normal, 5–15 mild, 15–30 moderate, >30 severe); obstructive apnea = effort continues, central = effort stops.

### 1.6 Methodology rules (non-negotiable, `CLAUDE.md` §8)
1 subject-independent evaluation always; 2 per-minute and per-recording reported separately; 3 beat the baseline or justify it; 4 start simple (classical → 1D-CNN → CNN-LSTM only if plateaued; no Transformer to start); 5 never train on our own recordings (**exception §1.4**); 6 reconcile 100 Hz vs 240 Hz (RR in seconds); 7 treat suspiciously perfect numbers as a bug (project flag: accuracy > 0.95 → check subject leakage first); 8 verify the true sample rate empirically.

### 1.7 Ethics / data handling
Human-subject recordings (`data/recordings/`, `data/mesa/`) are gitignored and never committed; outputs are anonymised (`S01..`, MESA ids only in gitignored files). **[FLAG]** several committed diagnostic artifacts still contain in-house recording *file names* (`results/inhouse_validation/quality_report.csv`, `README.md`, `plausibility/per_subject_predictions.csv`, `hudson_debug/*`) and the breath-hold file name appears in code (`src/breath_hold/analyze_session.py::REC_PATH`, `src/deploy/predict_session.py` via `trained_on` in `results/transfer/deployed_heads_meta.json`). Those predate or sit outside the later anonymisation rule (`results/inhouse_canet/README.md`: "no names/ids in any output"); they are recorded here, not changed.

### 3.1 §7d — Four label-free domain-adaptation attempts (belt → accelerometer)
**Common setting.** Frozen MESA-trained headline CANet (`data/mesa/cache/final_models/headline.pt`); effort-encoder tokens (75 × 64 per epoch). Belt side: 60 MESA subjects × 30 epochs = 1,800 epochs. In-house side: 10 usable nights ≈ 1,811 epochs (embedding study). Adaptors trained on nights **S03/S06/S07/S08/S11/S12/S13/S15 (2,411 epochs)**, held-out nights **S04, S10** (never trained on). No labels used anywhere. Reference flag rate before adaptation ~93–97% (median 0.9642 in the CORAL token run); MESA low-AHI reference 0.28.

**Step 1 — embedding-gap characterisation** (`src/adaptation/embed_gap.py`; `results/adaptation/embed_gap/` — `embeddings.npz`, `embedding_space.png`, `pca_dim_gap.png`, `run.log`; commit `19186b1`). Mechanism: PCA (50 dims) + t-SNE on the joint 128-d (GAP+GMP) embedding. Numbers: PC1 carries **82.4%** of variance; belt embeddings span the full PC1 range (−10…+35), accelerometer embeddings collapse into a tight cluster at the left end (−8…−2); mean shift only **0.162 SD** across 50 dims (top dims PC4 1.31 SD, PC3 0.84, PC9 0.67) yet separability 0.997; mean L2 distance MESA→in-house **10.086** vs within-MESA **10.601** (ratio 0.95×). Conclusion: the gap is in manifold structure (variance collapse in PC1, distributional shape in minor PCs), not a mean offset; partial t-SNE overlap, so adaptation is not hopeless, but a linear shift/scale will not do.

**Step 2a — CORAL at the pooled 128-d level** (`src/adaptation/coral_adaptor.py`, superseded version in git history; `results/adaptation/coral/FINDINGS.md`, `coral_alignment.png`, `reg_sweep.png`, `coral_params.npz`, `flag_rate_after_coral.csv`; commit `b014688`). Mechanism: closed-form covariance alignment (Sun et al. 2016) of accelerometer to belt pooled embeddings, fed to a concat-style head, bypassing cross-attention. Numbers: domain AUROC **0.9938 → 0.2177** (near-perfect alignment) but flag rate **~93% → 100%**. Diagnosed reason: the head was trained on cross-attended tokens, not raw pooled embeddings; bypassing cross-attention gave it an input it never saw — "alignment worked, model broke".

**Step 2b — CORAL at the token level** (64-d, pre-attention, full forward pass preserved; `coral_params_token.npz`, `reg_sweep_token.png`, `flag_rate_token_coral.csv`, `summary.json`). Numbers: domain AUROC **0.9542 → 0.9340** (best reg = 1.0 in a 0.01–10 sweep); median flag rate **0.9643** (per-night 0.893–0.979). Diagnosed reason: tokens carry temporal/positional structure; the gap is a *nonlinear* relationship between signal shape and token content (consistent with the spectral differences in §2.10), which a linear covariance transform cannot capture. Conclusion: linear alignment fails at either level.

**Step 3 — adversarial residual adaptor v1** (`src/adaptation/train_adaptor.py`; `results/adaptation/adaptor/` — `FINDINGS.md`, `adaptor_checkpoint.pt`, `training_curves.png`, `training_history.csv`, `validation_flag_rate.csv`, `summary.json`, `run.log`; commit `f01b8e4`). Mechanism: residual MLP per token (64→128→64, **16,576** adaptor parameters; discriminator 8,321), adversarial vs a belt/accelerometer discriminator + breathing-consistency loss (autocorrelation at the measured lag) + L2 residual anchor (0.05); 1,200 steps. Numbers: training unstable — residual loss rose 0.02 → 13.2 and oscillated ~1.6–7.4; discriminator confidence on belt tokens oscillated 0.46–0.78 (never the 0.5 equilibrium; final 0.634/0.381); final α 0.138; held-out flag rate S04 97%→84%, S10 ~96%→91.7% (median 0.878), "not credited as genuine improvement". **Reported held-out domain AUROC 1.0000 (vs ~0.93–0.95 pre-adaptation)** — read the erratum below. Diagnosed reason (as written): textbook adversarial overfitting to 8 nights' quirks. **ERRATUM (found 2026-10-09; recorded in `docs/PROGRESS.md` §7d Step 3 and as a pointer note at the top of `results/adaptation/adaptor/FINDINGS.md` in commit `33c586c`; the original FINDINGS text is unchanged):** `train_adaptor.py` lines ~310–322 fit the logistic regression and score it on the *same* data (`roc_auc_score(y, clf.predict_proba(X)[:,1])`) — the resubstitution error that attempt 2 identified and fixed, where it returned 1.0 even for the identity adaptor. The v1 "1.0000" is therefore uninterpretable and not valid evidence of overfitting; what stands for v1 is the unstable training dynamics and the absence of a credible flag-rate improvement.

**Step 4 — constrained adversarial adaptor v2** (`src/adaptation/train_adaptor_v2.py`; `results/adaptation/adaptor_v2/` — `FINDINGS.md`, `adaptor_checkpoint_best.pt`, `training_curves.png`, `training_history.csv`, `validation_flag_rate.csv`, `summary.json`; commit `4293788`). Numbers (post measurement fix, proper random split): identity-adaptor sanity check **0.9984**; best held-out AUROC during training **0.9981**; final at early stop (step 240) **0.9997**; α 0.050 → 0.057; held-out flag rate S04 85.8%, S10 92.3% (median 0.890). Verdict: **NO IMPROVEMENT** — stable but inert (the residual penalty kept it near identity). **Overall conclusion (as recorded).** Four distinct mechanisms — linear and nonlinear, loosely and tightly constrained — gave null or negative results: representation-space manipulation of the effort encoder's tokens cannot close the gap with 2,411 unlabelled epochs across 8 nights. Remaining routes: more in-house accelerometer data, a labelled supervisory signal (breath-holds — §2.12/§2.13), or retraining/fine-tuning the effort encoder on accelerometer data.

### 3.3 §7f — Why RMS-only fails (label impurity)
`results/transfer/results.json`: RMS-only pooled AUROC 0.599, sensitivity 0.0, per-fold min 0.0 (Event 1). Diagnosis (computed from `src/inhouse/streams.py` + `epoch_features` on the window index): a 30 s centre epoch cannot isolate a 19–21 s hold — every positive epoch contains 9–15 s of surrounding breathing including the inhale before and the recovery gasp after. Mean RMS of the 30 s positive epoch vs rest (normalised trace): Event 1 1.87 vs 1.13 (positives *higher*), Event 2 1.07 vs 1.25, Event 3 1.27 vs 1.37, Event 4 0.75 vs 1.74, Event 5 1.61 vs 2.02, Event 6 0.88 vs 1.91, Event 7 0.77 vs 2.40; baseline-period mean 1.49. So RMS is clearly lower for holds only in Events 4, 6, 7. Recorded as the same family of problem as the Phase 4 over-smoothed envelope and the §7e boundary bug (a window or boundary not matching the physical event's extent); it also handicaps the hand-crafted head's absolute level (out-of-fold sensitivity 0.43). The labels are impure by design (the frozen model's native input forces ≥ 30 s targets), not a measurement error to tune away.

### 3.4 Other negative / sobering results (all documented)
- Phase 2 per-recording metric (26/35) is not fixable by threshold choice (overlap of groups). Phase 3 per-recording stayed 28/35 across variants.
- Phase 3 Variant B (1.5× loss alone) is a net loss (0.775 → 0.758 accuracy); v2 did not compound with the amplitude channel.
- §4b: frequency-domain HRV is the weakest scalar bank (0.672); raw-ECG spectrogram (method 8) is the weakest overall (0.534, spec 0.448); CWT (method 7) swings to spec 0.911 / sens 0.491. QRS-area, expected to be a fallback EDR, has a worse QVAR domain gap (~268×) than amplitude (~108×).
- §7: the frozen PhysioNet ECG model predicts 52.8% apnea minutes on healthy QVAR recordings (21.3% RR-only); two hudson files stay implausible even RR-only (45 bpm physiology).
- §7c: the MESA-trained CANet flags 94% of healthy in-house epochs; effort side dominates.
- §7f: frozen features fail Events 5 and 7 (AUROC 0.16, 0.69) and flag 24% of the baseline period; no demonstrated benefit over 8 hand-crafted features.

## 4. Every bug found and fixed, with root cause

A — **Phase 3 undertrained first run** (`docs/PROGRESS.md` §3–4): the first CNN used 15 epochs; the loss curve and a train-set check showed 68% train accuracy and far fewer predicted apnea minutes than the true rate even on training data. Fix: 30 epochs (config of every adopted result). Verified by re-running; adopted numbers in §2.4.
B — **Delta print bug** (Phase 3 v1): printed `cnn_accuracy` instead of `cnn_accuracy - baseline_accuracy`, producing "+0.775 point improvement"; caught as implausibly large before reporting.
C — **Phase 4 over-smoothed cessation input**: the first synthetic test detected 1 of 3 real gaps. Root cause: `detect_cessations` was fed the ~10 s-smoothed `effort_amplitude` whose window is comparable to the 10–20 s events and blurred edges (a 15 s gap measured ~8.6 s, below the 10 s threshold). Investigated at the failing signal values rather than loosening the threshold. Fix: run on the raw (unsmoothed) Hilbert envelope; both docstrings warn about it. Verified: 3/3 real gaps detected, sub-threshold gap ignored (§2.7).
D — **Feature-study fairness (design) error**: scalar HRV banks (methods 3/4/5) were first forced through the 1D-CNN as a pseudo-sequence, handicapping them. Fix: added logistic regression and an MLP (`cnn_model.py::FeatureMLP`), report the best classifier per bank and name it (logreg won all three; method 4 spec 0.293 → 0.772). Commits `52f6fc8`…`6f6d936`.
E — **scipy API removal**: `scipy.signal.cwt`/`morlet2` are absent in the pinned scipy; method 7 uses PyWavelets (complex Morlet).
F — **Hudson/Hari misdiagnosis avoided**: the hypothesis "R-peak under-detection" was tested and rejected (§2.8) — recorded as a process win; actual causes were physiology and an electrical artifact.
G — **SensorTile loader format bug** (`68d16f8`, 2026-10-08): the 2026-10 firmware changed the output to comma-delimited `.csv` with header `time[us]` and renamed columns (`acc_x[mg]`, `ah_qvar[LSB]`, `gyro_*[dsp]`); the old loader looked for `Timestamp` and tab separation and could not read the new file. Discovered when the first breath-hold recording was loaded. Fix in `src/ingest/sensortile.py`: `_find_header_row` accepts `Timestamp` or `time[us]`; `_is_new_csv_format` checks `.csv` + comma-delimited `time[us]` header; both formats normalise to the same `SensorTileRecord`. Verified (commit message): regression on the old `.txt` (`user_kishor0404_ecg_000.txt` reproduces the `quality_report.csv` numbers) and the new CSV loads (243.10 Hz, 29.93 min, 0 gaps, accelerometer usable). **[FLAG]** the code comment and commit message refer to "Sec.11.1 of the original handoff doc / `PROGRESS.md` Sec.11.1", which does not exist in the repository as it stands.
H — **Resubstitution-AUROC measurement bug in the adaptors** (§7d): `held_out_domain_auroc` fit a logistic regression and scored it on the same data, giving AUROC 1.0 even for the identity adaptor. Discovered because the identity sanity check returned 1.0 ("impossible for a generalisation measurement"). Fix in `train_adaptor_v2.py` (lines ~145–170): random train/test split inside the function; post-fix identity = 0.9984. **Not fixed in `train_adaptor.py` (v1)** — its reported 1.0000 is uninterpretable (§3.1); the experiment is closed, so the bug is documented by an erratum (`33c586c`) rather than re-run.
I — **R-peak spike despiking** (`analyze_session.py` v2): HR-based boundary refinement latched onto isolated single-beat R-peak detection artifacts (~150 bpm jumps without buildup, also present outside hold windows). Fix: `despike_hr` drops a beat whose HR differs from *both* neighbours by > 40 bpm (2 / 2,159 beats removed). **Residual gap:** a two-beat spike near 1000 s in Event 1 (~153 bpm) is not isolated and survives; it inflates that event's hold-mean HR (+5.46 bpm).
J — **Effort-ratio computed on nominal boundaries after refinement** (v1 → v2): a recovery gasp leaking past the nominal boundary inflated the ratio (Event 7). Fix: all summary statistics use one set of boundaries consistently (and the final version reports all three windows explicitly).
K — **Effort boundary bug (signed signal, 20 ms runs)**: root cause and fix in §3.2. Verified fixed at the level of "no longer edge-pinned" (start offsets −6.3…+1.4 s for refined events) but the detector remains unreliable (3/7 visually correct).
L — **§7f leakage purge** (a design-time fix, not a bug found after the fact): CANet inputs are 150 s, so windows around the same hold share raw samples; a window- or even event-only split leaks. Fix: leave-one-**event**-out with training windows whose full 150 s input overlaps the held-out hold ± 15 s purged, plus windows overlapping test epochs. Verified: folds have 24 training positives from the 6 other events, 4–8 purged (`results/transfer/fold_sizes.csv`); the shuffled-training-label null gives 0.484 / 0.446 (no label leakage); no pooled AUROC > 0.95 (but see the Event-5 dominance note). N — **`PROGRESS.md` duplication** (cleaned in `1b0b674`): §7d appeared three times, the §8 table had triplicate rows, the §9 directory map had duplicate `adaptation/` blocks.

## 7. Every open question / unresolved item

1. **HR direction during holds is unconfirmed** — the sign of hold-minus-pre HR flips with boundary choice (v1 +0.84 bpm, 5/7 up; v2 −3.08, 1/7 up; final −1.35, SD 4.8, 3/7 up). No bradycardia/tachycardia claim is made; voluntary awake holds may show anticipatory sympathetic rise (hypothesis only). Event 1's two-beat ~153 bpm artifact survives the despiker.
2. **Automatic boundary detection is unresolved** — three detector designs; 3/7 events visually correct (2, 4, 6); needs a second session with sync taps/event markers; effort ratio is a per-event range (inner → wide).
3. **Event 7 padding vs event-specific cause is unresolved** — the frozen features fail Event 7 (AUROC 0.688, sens 0.00) with up to 27% zero-padded input; a padding control on events 1–6 did not degrade them (0.840 → 0.892) and Event 5 (no padding) fails equally; the two explanations are confounded across Event 7's 4 windows. Not decided either way.
4. **Temporal drift is not excluded (open limitation, §7f)** — baseline-period windows (first ~900 s) vs training windows (last ~800 s); the 24% (frozen) vs 0.5% (hand-crafted) false-positive gap could partly reflect drift in posture, contact, motion or HR state; the shuffled-label control does not test it. Needs a second session with holds spread across the recording and a rest-only control stretch.
5. **Frozen vs hand-crafted comparison is not apples-to-apples** (150 s cardiac + effort vs 30 s effort); neither a hand-crafted 150 s baseline nor a frozen 30 s effort-only run exists.
6. **Pilot scale** — n = 7 events, one subject, one session; pooled CIs 0.58–1.00 (frozen) / 0.71–0.97 (hand-crafted); the frozen cardiac-half score (0.865) is unexplained and not claimed as a cardiac effect; five feature sets × four C values are extra looks (only frozen-256 vs hand-crafted-8 at C = 0.01 was pre-specified); with weaker regularisation hand-crafted leads (0.92 at C = 1).
7. **Label impurity** from 30 s target epochs vs 19–21 s holds (§3.3).
8. **LSTM/RNN (§7g)** — not run, by reasoning (sample size/non-interpretable either way). Conditions to revisit (scaling estimates from CI widths): ≈ 30+ independent labelled holds (≈ 4× today), ≥ 10 subjects for subject-independent evaluation, repeat sessions with sync taps and the SHORT-hold test, a validated boundary detector, a baseline to beat (hand-crafted per-fold minimum 0.78), and fine-tuning of the effort encoder rather than only a head once the labelled set is large. Open to the advisor's input.
9. **§7d attempt-1 erratum** — "held-out AUROC 1.0000" is a resubstitution artifact; erratum recorded in §7d and as a pointer in `results/adaptation/adaptor/FINDINGS.md` (`33c586c`). Still repeating the invalid comparison, left as historical record: `results/adaptation/adaptor_v2/FINDINGS.md` ("Change from attempt 1" paragraph) and the §7d summary text "worse than the pre-adaptation baseline".
10. **No labelled in-house data beyond the one session**; the SHORT 5–7 s hold false-positive test was never recorded; no rest-period-vs-hold "general stillness" control (listed in v1 FINDINGS as a next step).
11. **Whether the accelerometer can detect cessation at sleep-apnea timescales/physiology** is untested (voluntary awake holds only).
12. **Subject-independent validation of anything on our hardware** is impossible with one subject.
13. **Sample-rate reconciliation** (100 vs ~243 Hz) is by construction (time units; RR in seconds; per-night normalisation) — it has run on real data (all in-house tracks) but was never isolated as its own experiment.
14. **QVAR amplitude recalibration** and an **ECG-domain signal-quality gate** (found in §7) are not built; bradycardia (~45 bpm) generalisation gap of the PhysioNet-trained model.
15. **MESA is an elderly cohort** (mean ~68, `docs/MESA_NOTES.md`); transfer to 19–20-year-olds is a stated limitation; no SpO2 on the wearable → hypopnea limitation to be named in any per-night report.
16. **Cardiac side of CANet on healthy hearts is mildly elevated (58% flagged)** — cause (respiratory sinus arrhythmia) is unverified.
17. **Stale documents:** `README.md` status and `CLAUDE.md` §11/§13 were updated in `5ebf3e3` (original text kept under dated notes); still stale: `CLAUDE.md` §14 (phases 4–7 "need hardware", partly overtaken), `docs/presentation_handoff/*` (predates §7d–§7g), `docs/PROGRESS.md` §3–4 Phase 4 text and §6 limitations ("no own-hardware data") which later sections supersede. **[FLAG]** also `PROGRESS.md` §7c's "Whether our accelerometer can even detect cessation is now the critical open question" is partly answered by §7e.
