Limitations

  • Healthy 19-20-year-old volunteers: no apnea accuracy can be claimed on our hardware. Voluntary breath-holds are not clinical apnea.
  • ECG models: PhysioNet-trained; the R-peak-amplitude channel does not transfer to QVAR (see RR-only column).
  • Effort heads: pilot, 1 subject, 7 holds (PROGRESS.md §7f). Hypopnea cannot be assessed without SpO2.
