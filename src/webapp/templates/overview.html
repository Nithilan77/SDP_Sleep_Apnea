Detecting sleep apnea from a chest-worn ECG + motion sensor

One chest SensorTile records ECG (via QVAR) and chest-wall motion (IMU). Apnea is a cardiorespiratory event, so the project asks whether fusing heart and breathing signals beats the heart alone. Models are trained on public, expert-labelled clinical data; our own healthy-volunteer recordings are used only for validation.

Pipeline

1 · SensorSensorTile.box PRO: QVAR as chest ECG @ ~243 Hz + IMU

4 · FusionDecision-level; validated only on MESA belts
