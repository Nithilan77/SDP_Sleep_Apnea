Ensembles, expanded data and in-house breath-hold results

Three separate result families — never pooled. A · public sleep-apnea data (PhysioNet/MESA, subject-independent protocols). B · in-house voluntary breath-holds (one volunteer, 7 events, leave-one-event-out; feasibility only, not apnea). S · simulator experiments (ECG classifier trained with simulator/augmented minutes, always tested on real held-out PhysioNet records). Synthetic-only scores are never shown as real-world performance.
