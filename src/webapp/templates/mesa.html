Reading the ablation

  • Respiratory effort carries most of the signal: ECG-only is the weakest configuration, and every configuration containing effort or SpO2 beats it by a wide margin.
  • SpO2 alone is already competitive with ECG + effort on AUROC. That supports using an oximeter when one is available, but SpO2 is not measured by our device, so this result cannot be used to claim anything about our hardware.
  • Adding SpO2 to ECG + effort gives the best AUROC; whether that increment is meaningful should be judged against the fold-to-fold SD (~0.015–0.023 AUROC).
  • These are associations from ablations of one architecture family, single seed per fold. They do not establish that any modality is causally necessary, and ECG's contribution in the fused model is smaller than effort's.
