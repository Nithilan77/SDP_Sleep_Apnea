Methodology & limitations

Datasets & provenance

  • PhysioNet Apnea-ECG: 35 labelled records (a01–a20, b01–b05, c01–c10), single-lead ECG at 100 Hz, per-minute expert apnea labels. Training and evaluation of the ECG models.
  • MESA via NSRR: 250 subjects downloaded, 220 pass the usable-signal funnel; ECG (256 Hz), thoracic/abdominal belts (32 Hz), SpO2 (1 Hz), event labels.
  • In-house SensorTile.box PRO logs (QVAR-ECG + IMU, ~243 Hz measured): healthy volunteers; raw files are never committed. Under the policy revised 2026-10-20 they may be used for training/fine-tuning/augmentation with strict separation and are reported separately from the public results; before that date they were not trained on, except linear heads on 7 labelled holds of one session, documented in docs/PROGRESS.md §7f).

Pipeline & models

  • ECG → R-peaks → per-minute RR-interval and R-amplitude sequence (60 steps) → 1D-CNN (3.4k params), LSTM/GRU, TCN, or tabular HRV/CVHR features.
  • Effort: accelerometer → 0.08–0.6 Hz band → Hilbert envelope → hold-likelihood from two linear heads (pilot).
  • MESA: CANet — multi-scale CNN encoders per stream with bidirectional cross-modal attention (169k params for ECG + effort).

Validation & leakage prevention

  • Subject-independent only: each Apnea-ECG record is a subject (leave-one-record-out); MESA uses subject-grouped 5-fold CV.
  • Normalisation, class weights, early stopping and decision thresholds are computed from training/validation partitions only; the held-out subject is used once.
  • Historical runs did not fully meet this: the CNN variant was chosen on test folds; RNN/LSTM v2 configs were selected on records that are also test folds. The controlled comparison on the model page removes this and is the unbiased estimate.
  • Metrics: accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, precision, F1, AUROC, AUPRC, confusion matrices, per-recording correctness (record flagged if >5% of minutes positive). Per-minute and per-recording are different tasks.

Negative / limiting results (kept on purpose)

  • Belt → accelerometer transfer failed: four domain-adaptation attempts (CORAL pooled/token, adversarial v1/v2) did not close the gap; frozen MESA features tied hand-crafted effort features on pooled AUROC (0.83 vs 0.83) but were worse per event and gave more false positives (n = 7 events, one subject).
  • RNN/LSTM did not beat the CNN in the historical runs; see the controlled comparison for the multi-seed result.
  • The ECG R-amplitude channel does not transfer from PhysioNet to QVAR (≈100× scale mismatch); healthy-volunteer flag rates are therefore inflated with the amplitude channel.
  • SpO2 and EEG are not recorded by our wearable; MESA SpO2 results do not apply to it.

Clinical status

This is a research prototype for screening-style flagging. It is not a diagnostic device, has no clinical validation, cannot assess hypopnea without SpO2, and was never evaluated on patients with our hardware.
