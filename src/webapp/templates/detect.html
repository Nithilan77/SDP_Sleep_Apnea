Apnea detection · upload & inference

Upload a SensorTile.box PRO log whose QVAR channel is wired as chest ECG. The server validates it, detects R-peaks, and runs the PhysioNet-trained ECG models and the pilot accelerometer effort heads. No labels exist for your file, so outputs are flag rates, not accuracy.

Accepted: SensorTile .csv (columns time[us], acc_*[mg], gyro_*[dsp], ah_qvar[LSB]) or legacy tab-delimited .txt; ≥ 10 min; measured rate 180–300 Hz; QVAR must show a cardiac rhythm (spectral prominence ≥ {{20}}). EMG/EOG-wired tiles, IMU-only logs and other formats are rejected with the reason. Processing is synchronous (10–60 s; overnight files longer).

Limitations

  • Healthy 19–20-year-old volunteers: no apnea accuracy can be claimed on our hardware; voluntary breath-holds are not clinical apnea.
  • ECG models are PhysioNet-trained; the R-amplitude channel does not transfer to QVAR.
  • Hypopnea cannot be assessed without SpO2. This is a screening research prototype, not a diagnosis.
