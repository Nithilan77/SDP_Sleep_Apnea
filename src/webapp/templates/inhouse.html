What this establishes: in one volunteer, the chest-accelerometer breathing-band amplitude is lower in the conservative hold windows in 7/7 events (descriptive).
What it does not: a validated breath-hold detector; reproducibility (session 2 could not be aligned and its ECG-tile accelerometer shows no breathing signal); generalisation (n = 1 volunteer, 7 holds); or anything about clinical apnea.

Likely limitations & what would fix them

  • Sensor coupling differs between tiles and sessions (session-2 ECG-tile accelerometer is ~28× quieter than session 1's), and body position of each tile is not logged.
  • No shared clock or sync taps across the three tiles of a session in session 2, so cross-tile alignment is impossible after the fact.
  • Fix for the next session: one tile on the chest with ECG and IMU, logged start-to-end with sync taps, a written event log, and a recorded placement.
