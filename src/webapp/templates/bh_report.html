Known behaviour

Leave-one-event-out on session 1 (seed 0, whole-recording scan): 4/7 holds detected, 6 additional flagged segments (~12 per hour); quiet-breathing valleys are the main false flags. Augmentation + simulator training detected 5/7 with 22 extra segments and was not deployed.

Limitations

  • {{r.meta.caveat}}
  • Breath-holds are voluntary and are not clinical apnea; low-effort windows can also be quiet breathing or stillness.
  • Exploratory feasibility output, one volunteer, no independent validation.
