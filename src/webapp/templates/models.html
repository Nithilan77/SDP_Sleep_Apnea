Model comparison

Task: per-minute apnea vs normal (PhysioNet Apnea-ECG expert labels) from single-lead ECG, 35 records, leave-one-subject-out (LOSO). This page compares models on that one task only; MESA results use a different task and dataset and live on the MESA page.

Controlled comparison · new

Same folds, same protocol, 3 seeds, no test-fold selection

Per outer fold, 5 training records are held out (seeded) for early stopping (best validation AUROC) and for the decision threshold (max balanced accuracy). Normalisation and class weights use the remaining training records only; the test record is used once. Hyperparameters were fixed before running. Values are mean ± SD over seeds of pooled per-minute metrics.
