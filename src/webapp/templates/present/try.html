How to read this

  • A flagged stretch means the chest-motion pattern looks like a breathing pause. It is a screening hint.
  • A low flagged share means this model saw few pause-like patterns in this recording. It does not prove that breathing was normal, and it is not a diagnosis.
  • A high share can also come from the sensor not sitting on the chest, a lot of movement, or the sensor differences described on the overview page.
  • The heads learned from the breath-holds of one volunteer, so treat this as a feasibility demonstration.
